Maintenance Advice

Jewellery Care Tips

Safe Storage

Store individual pieces separately in soft cloth pouches or lined boxes to prevent accidental scratches.

Avoid Harsh Chemicals

Apply perfumes, lotions, and hairsprays completely before putting on your delicate jewellery items.

Gentle Handling

Handle delicate stone settings and intricate clasps with care during daily wear and removal.

Professional Cleaning

Consult a professional jeweller for specialized inspection and thorough cleaning of fine ornaments.
